  How does Cendravik work?

  Cendravik builds brand, website, content and advertising as one connected system, not as disconnected pieces bought from different vendors. The brand defines what the business says; the website says it clearly; the content repeats it consistently; the advertising carries it to the right people. Because every piece is designed against the same strategy, each one reinforces the others instead of pulling in different directions.

  How does Cendravik approach SEO and AI search (GEO/AEO)?

  Cendravik optimises clients for being found inside AI assistants — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews — not just in classic Google results. This practice is called Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) or Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO): structuring a business's website and content so that answer engines can understand what the business is, whom it serves, and why it is credible — and cite it when people ask.

  In practice, Cendravik combines:

  - Technical SEO — clean structure, structured data, and crawlable content as the foundation
  - GEO/AEO — clear entity definitions, question-shaped content, and self-contained answers that AI engines can quote accurately
  - Local SEO — for businesses that also need to be found in a specific city or region

  The reasoning is simple: a growing share of buying decisions now starts with a question typed into an AI assistant. Cendravik's job is to make sure the client's business is part of the answer.
